A Confession From Your Little Brother

Honest Words From My Heart...

Dear Didi,

You know how much I look up to you. Since I don’t have a biological sister, you are literally my entire world when it comes to having a sister. I think about you almost every single day—wondering how your classes are going, if you’re eating properly, and wishing I could just teleport to where you are.

"Now, let me be completely honest like a typical protective younger brother... Hearing that you made a boyfriend in college honestly made me feel a little jealous! Not because I don't want you to be happy, of course I want all the happiness in the universe for you—but because when you're my only Didi, sharing your time and attention feels tough for your little brother!"

I just want you to always remember one thing: college friends and relationships have their own place, and I genuinely pray he treats you with the utmost respect you deserve. But nobody on this planet can ever replace the pure, unbreakable bond between a Didi and her Chota Bhai.

No matter how busy college gets, don't ever forget the boy in Kota who found his sister in you.

Forever your Chota Bhai ❤️
